Game, 2026, solo

THE WHIP

Godot 4.7GDScriptPythonsimulationplaytest bots

A 2D physics platformer where a simulated whip is your only tool and your only way to move. Throw it at a branch, hold, step off the edge, and the drop is your swing; let go on the way up and fly.

  • The whip is the physics. A hand-written chain of 26 particles at 60 Hz with 16 substeps, no engine physics and no random numbers, so every run is replayable bit for bit.
  • Playtesting as measurement. Bots throw every stroke of a 36-stroke grid from every ledge. A level ships only when at least three strokes land with a release window a person can hit. The demo's climbing stages failed that bar with the branch 1.0 m up and passed at 1.25 m, so that is where the branch is.
  • Feel decisions were prototyped, not argued. Three control schemes and nine feel options were built as switchable modes and measured side by side. The losers stayed in the record, including a jump that measured as making the swing worse.
  • The demo run is a regression test. The GIF is a replay of a recorded run through the real input path; if the simulation moves by a bit, the run stops landing and the test fails.

Product Lens

PythonFastAPIOpen Food Factsrules in YAML50 tests

Scan a food barcode and get told what on the label deserves scepticism, with the regulation, report or guidance note that says so. Not a nutrition score: the gap between what a label implies and what it legally guarantees.

  • The rules are the product. 23 of them in one readable file: unregulated terms such as "all natural", the six colours that must carry a hyperactivity warning, olive-oil grade checks, honey origin wording. Every rule carries a verbatim quote and a link to its source, and the loader refuses to start on a rule it cannot defend.
  • Posture decided by the market and the case law. Yuka won on appeal where its flags were sourced and measured and lost outright where it generalised without evidence. So the app never says a product is bad: it says what a word means, what a category has been found to contain, or what is missing.
  • AI reads, people judge. A model may transcribe a pack photo or propose a quote from a document; a human promotes every rule. Known barcodes cost nothing to look up.
  • Honest about coverage. Built on crowd-sourced data, thin on Irish own-brand shelves, and an unknown barcode says so and offers a link to add it.

Review-aware hotel recommender

Final-year project, UCD, 2026 · Python, SQL/MySQL, pandas, parquet, OpenAI API

An item-to-item recommender over roughly 2,370 hotels and 165,000+ TripAdvisor reviews. Reviews converted from MySQL to parquet and enriched with aspect-level sentiment features; four rankers (sentiment similarity, amenity overlap, contextual popularity, weighted hybrid) evaluated offline across relevance, coverage, city match, sentiment alignment and rating. Frequency-based ranking led on the relevance proxy; the sentiment-enhanced ranker returned higher-rated hotels with closer review-aspect alignment.
